# Quick Reference Guide

## Field Representative — Completing a Form

CCHP Provider Form App | Cook Children's Health Plan

---

## At a Glance

| Step | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Open the app → select a form |
| 2 | Answer each section and tap **Next** |
| 3 | On the last section, tap **Review & Download** |
| 4 | Review your answers, then tap **Download Completed PDF** |

---

## Step 1 — Pick Your Form

1. Open the app. The **Form Library** shows all available forms.
2. Tap the form card you need:

   - **Provider Visit 2026** — for provider office visit documentation
   - **Site Evaluation Tool** — for provider site compliance evaluations

> **Draft in progress?** If you started a form previously, the card will show an amber "Draft in progress" badge.
> - Tap **Continue Draft** to pick up where you left off.
> - Tap **Start Fresh** to clear the draft and begin again.

---

## Step 2 — Complete Each Section

### Navigation

- The **blue progress bar** at the top shows which section you are on (e.g., "2 of 8") and the section name.
- Tap **Next →** to advance to the next section.
- Tap **← Back** to go to the previous section (or return to the Form Library from section 1).

### Saving Your Work

- Your answers are **auto-saved automatically** every time you make a change. You can close the browser and come back later — your draft will still be there.
- Tap the **Save** button in the bottom bar anytime to confirm a manual save. It will flash **"Saved!"** green for 2 seconds.

### Question Types

| Type | How to Answer |
| --- | --- |
| **Text / Email / Phone / Date** | Tap the field and type your answer |
| **Single Choice** | Tap one button — it highlights blue when selected. Tap again to deselect |
| **Multiple Choice (checkboxes)** | Tap any number of buttons — each highlights with a checkmark when selected |
| **Long Text** | Tap the large text area and type your response |
| **Yes / No / NA (scored)** | See below |

### Yes / No / NA Scored Tables (Site Evaluation Tool)

- Each row is one evaluation item.
- Tap **Yes**, **No**, or **NA** for each row.
  - **Yes** = green — earns the listed points
  - **No** = red — zero points for this item
  - **NA** = gray — item does not apply; not counted
- The **Pts** column on the right updates live as you answer.
- The section score (e.g., "6/10 pts") shows in the top-right corner of the section.

### Conditional Questions

Some questions only appear when a previous answer triggers them. For example:

- Selecting **"Other"** in Items Discussed reveals a text field to describe the other item.
- Selecting **"Unaccepted Review"** reveals a Revisit Date field.

These will appear automatically — you do not need to do anything special.

---

## Step 3 — Review Your Answers

After the last section, the app takes you to the **Review** screen.

- All sections are listed with collapsible panels.
- Each section shows how many questions you answered (e.g., "7/9 answered").
- **Scored sections** show earned/possible points (e.g., "8/10 pts").
- The blue **summary banner** at the top shows your **Total Score** if the form has scored sections.

### Need to fix something?

Tap the **Edit** button on any section. You will be taken back to that specific section. Make your changes, then tap **Next** through the remaining sections to return to Review.

---

## Step 4 — Download the PDF

1. Tap **Download Completed PDF** at the bottom of the Review screen.
2. The button will show **"Generating PDF…"** for a moment.
3. Your browser will automatically download the completed, formatted CCHP PDF.
4. The file name will be: `[FormName]_[YYYY-MM-DD].pdf`
   - Example: `Provider_Visit_2026_2026-04-21.pdf`

After the download, you will see a **"PDF downloaded successfully!"** confirmation.

- Tap **New Form** to start a fresh copy of the same form.
- Tap **Back to Form Library** to return to the home screen.

---

## Tips & Reminders

- **No internet required** for PDF generation — everything runs in the browser.
- **Your data never leaves your device.** All answers are stored locally.
- If you accidentally close the app, reopen it and tap **Continue Draft** — your answers are still there.
- Required fields are marked with a red asterisk **\***. You can still advance sections without completing them, but they will show as "Not answered" on the Review screen.
- The PDF is formatted to match the official CCHP paper form exactly — no reformatting needed before filing.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
| --- | --- |
| My draft is gone | Browser storage may have been cleared. Always download the PDF before clearing browser data |
| PDF did not download | Check your browser's download folder or pop-up blocker settings |
| A question I expected is missing | It may be conditional — check if its parent question is answered |
| The form loads but appears blank | Tap the form title/back and re-enter; reload the page if needed |
